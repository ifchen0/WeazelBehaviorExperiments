Small experimental behaviours for the XCC faction of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3820790624]Weazel Mode[/url].

These scenarios are my own take on how the Weazels would act, based on my impression of the faction. They are not part of the original mod's lore and are not endorsed by its author.

[h2]Protection racket[/h2]
While your colony is neutral with XCC, a trade caravan from a faction hostile to XCC may draw an XCC strike team onto your map (30% per caravan by default). The team hunts the caravan, and once the caravan is gone, defeated or left, it collects its due before leaving. XCC treats a neutral colony as its own turf.

[h2]Wasted time[/h2]
An XCC squad called in with favor that leaves without taking any damage collects compensation on the way out. They feel you wasted their time.

[h2]Ignored ceremony[/h2]
Letting an XCC bestowing ceremony expire costs goodwill with XCC (15 by default). It never drops goodwill low enough to turn XCC hostile, since a war would break out with their officer still on your map.

[h2]Notes[/h2][list]
[*]Collecting uses the vanilla raider stealing behaviour: each member grabs one valuable item within a short distance, or leaves empty-handed. A defeated caravan's dropped goods are the usual catch.
[*]A message in the top-left corner explains each event when it starts (no letters).
[*]Attacking the team turns XCC hostile, as with any neutral visitor.
[*]The caravan chance, the wasted-time behaviour and the ceremony goodwill loss can be changed in the mod settings.
[*]Safe to add to an existing save. Remove it only when no XCC strike team or collecting squad is on the map.
[/list]
